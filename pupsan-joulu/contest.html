Pupsa's Outfit Contest: Official Rules

Organizer

Polar Pine Apps, the developer name of Jussi Reiss, a private individual in Finland.

jussi@polarpineapps.com

Entry period

From the publication of the contest video on TikTok until November 20, 2026 at 21:00 Finnish time.

How to enter

Comment your outfit idea on the contest video on @polarpineapps. Entry is free. You do not need to buy anything, download the game or follow the account. You may post several ideas, and each comment is judged separately.

Who can enter

Anyone with a TikTok account. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before entering.

Your idea

It must be your own and suitable for a family game. Do not include brands, logos, or characters from films, games, shows or books. Polar Pine Apps may hide or remove comments that are offensive, unsafe for a family audience or spam.

Winner

The eligible comment with the most likes when the contest ends wins. If two comments tie, the earlier one wins. We may disqualify comments that break these rules or use fake likes. If a comment is disqualified, the next most-liked eligible comment wins.

If there are fewer than 10 eligible entries, or if like counts appear to have been manipulated, Polar Pine Apps picks the winner from the eligible entries based on creativity and fit with the game.

Announcement

We will announce the winner on TikTok by November 27, 2026 and reply to the winning comment. The winner must email us within 7 days. If they do not, the next most-liked eligible comment wins.

Prize

We will add the winning idea to Pupsa's Christmas as a wearable item in a future update. We may adapt the design to fit the game. With your permission, and a parent's or guardian's permission if you are under 18, we will show a nickname you choose with the item. The nickname must not be a real full name. The prize cannot be exchanged for cash. Pupsa's Christmas is coming soon to iPhone and Mac and has not been released yet.

Use of your idea

By entering, you allow Polar Pine Apps to use and adapt your idea in the game and its marketing, free of charge.

Personal data

We use the winner's contact details only to run this contest and deliver the prize.

This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or run by TikTok or Apple.
